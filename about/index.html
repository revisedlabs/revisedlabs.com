About

Revised Labs LLC is an independent consulting practice specializing in secure, scalable, and maintainable systems.

We partner with organizations at every stage, from early startups to established teams, bringing a pragmatic, long-term approach to system design and reliability.

Our roots go back more than 20 years to the early days of the game industry, before the modern internet era. At that time, everything was built in-house, and strong design patterns, performance awareness, and efficiency were essential to shipping successful products.

Over the years, we have evolved alongside the industry, adopting modern standards and best practices as they emerge. We build systems from the ground up when needed, or strategically leverage proven commercial and open-source technologies to accelerate delivery and maximize customer value.

At every engagement, we focus on translating business goals into clear technical requirements—and delivering solutions that are reliable, maintainable, and built to last.
